Research

The data side of understanding people.

My research experiences have taught me to move between quantitative analysis, program evaluation, clinical questions, and the stories people use to make sense of their lives.

Belmont Adolescent Partial Hospitalization Program

Clinical Research

I am currently working with another Post-Baccalaureate Fellow to investigate the effects of affective involvement and direct communication on internalizing behaviors among adolescents we treat, as well as the impact of increased levels of behavior control on externalizing behaviors.

We work with de-personalized data from previous patients at the BAPHP. I also participated in submitting an IRB proposal for an efficacy study examining the skills taught and groups led at the program, with the goal of better supporting patients and expanding opportunities for future research.

JAG Institute · Judi’s House

Childhood Bereavement

As an Evaluation and Research Intern, I worked closely with the JAG Institute team on an efficacy study and a technical assistance program. The efficacy study investigates the effectiveness of clinical programs, using data to continuously improve services.

The technical assistance program offers a guided course to childhood bereavement organizations to collect and use their own data more effectively to measure outcomes, impact, and influence future decision-making.

My work also brought me into direct contact with the clinical environment at Judi’s House, where research and clinical teams work together to support children and families.

Pitzer College · Spring 2026

The Study of Lives

The Study of Lives was a practicum-based narrative psychology course focused on the evolving stories we create to communicate who we are, how we became who we are, and what our future might hold.

Through a partnership with Mt. San Antonio Gardens, each student was matched with a resident and conducted extensive interviews covering their life up to the present. We analyzed the interviews for qualitative patterns, themes, and the impact of events and people across a resident’s life.

My resident and I spent nearly 15 hours together during the interview process, and the final compilation totaled more than 300 pages. It was a privilege to be immersed in someone else’s life in this way. This course forever changed how I view education.
