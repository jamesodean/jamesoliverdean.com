A way of working

Curiosity · Empathy · Growth

These principles guide my life, whether I’m on the unit, with my family, or in nature.

Curiosity

I work to embrace radical curiosity in every moment. Acting from a place of curiosity, rather than judgment, allows for more open-mindedness, greater connection with others, and a deeper sense of self.

Empathy

Empathy works hand-in-hand with curiosity. I work to understand where people are coming from, why they make the choices they make, and work together to find ways to create a life in which they find value.

Growth

I view human existence as an endless cycle of growth and learning. Every day is an opportunity to grow.
