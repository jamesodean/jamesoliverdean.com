Background

From research to direct work with people.

I studied psychology and religious studies at Pitzer College, exploring both the scientific study of human behavior and the broader questions surrounding meaning-making and the human experience.

My academic and professional experiences have taken me from psychological research to youth education and community engagement. I was the Research and Evaluation Intern at Judi’s House/JAG Institute, an organization in Denver, CO, that supports children and families grieving a death through counseling, training, and research.

I am particularly interested in opportunities that allow me to combine research, psychological science, and direct work with people.
